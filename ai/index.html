case file 01 · research · vlm · llm fine-tuning

Accuracy Without Grounding: Diagnosing and Repairing a Medical VLM

The problemA chest X-ray question-answering model gave fluent, plausible answers. Was it actually looking at the X-ray?
What I didBuilt six model variants, designed a blind-image control (swap the X-ray for matched noise, keep everything else), found the failure, traced it to unstable visual-token scale and repaired it with Layer Normalisation plus longer training. A 2×2 ablation showed neither change works alone.
16.6→81.0%real-image accuracy, before vs after repair
